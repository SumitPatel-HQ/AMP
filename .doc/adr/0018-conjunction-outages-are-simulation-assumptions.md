# Conjunction evidence describes a prediction and an assumed payload outage

We reuse `SATELLITE_UNAVAILABLE` with an optional, complete `conjunction` group. The event stores the source attribution, encounter identity, matched and counterpart NORAD identities, UTC TCA, miss distance in kilometers, source maximum probability, threshold, policy version, and positive pre/post padding in seconds. Evidence-free payloads keep their existing wire shape and validation. Accepted events are the replay unit (ADR-0002); neither planning nor reconstruction reads a source report or accesses the network.

`socrates-outage-v1` compares the supplied normalized maximum probability against an explicitly recorded threshold (the proposed default is 0.0001). That value is a simulation selection policy, not a measured collision outcome, operational advice, or proof of a real maneuver. Ticket E-01 uses explicitly synthetic evidence and makes no claim about SOCRATES report conversion or historical provenance; source representation, report integrity, availability dates, and archiving belong to E-02.

The author supplies an assumed half-open interval exactly equal to TCA minus pre-padding through TCA plus post-padding. MissionSession validates evidence, stored Satellite orbital identity, full horizon containment, injection no later than interval start, and duplicate encounter/Satellite identity before mutation. Distinct encounters remain separate records; the existing planner applies all intervals, giving their union without counting an imaging action twice. No orbit change, fuel use, delta-v, or maneuver confirmation is modeled.

`COLLISION_AVOIDANCE_OUTAGE` means recorded conjunction unavailability blocked the previous imaging placement. It is not inferred from arbitrary plan differences. An earlier manual outage and base unavailability retain their ordinary reason; unrelated constraint violations retain their reasons. A dropped action may report conjunction displacement when the remaining windows fail availability or timing, while resource failure keeps its ordinary reason. Traces link the overlapping recorded evidence and name counterparts. Frozen conflicts appear in Impact.reason_codes alongside frozen_action_ids, outside invalid_unfrozen_action_ids: replanning never moves, drops, or recosts frozen actions (ADR-0003).
